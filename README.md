# Meridian - AI Product Builder take-home

Start with [ASSIGNMENT.md](ASSIGNMENT.md). The data governance exports are in [`meridian_data/`](meridian_data/).

Designed for 90 minutes to two hours. If you run out of time, ship the narrow thing that works
rather than the broad thing that does not.

## How to submit

1. Clone this repo.
2. Build your solution in your OWN private repository. Please do not fork publicly and do not open
   PRs here - other candidates receive the same exercise.
3. When done, invite `Rogdrat` as a collaborator to your solution repo and send the link to your
   Improvado contact.

Questions about scope or ambiguity: make a reasonable assumption, write it down in one line, and
proceed - that is part of the exercise.
